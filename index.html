The Engagement

Six weeks. One module deep. A framework you can repeat.

Weeks 1–2

Discovery

I interview 5–8 people across engineering, product, and platform. I'm mapping the gap between what your platform team knows (the infrastructure) and what your product team knows (the users and the story). I identify where code review piles up, where Claude goes off the rails, and where institutional knowledge lives in one person's head.

Weeks 2–3

Audit & Architecture

I deliver a gap analysis: what knowledge exists, where it lives, what's missing. Then I draft the architecture for your documentation — product context, system rules, traversal maps, coding conventions — structured so both humans and AI agents can use it effectively.

Weeks 3–5

Build a Slice

I pick the area where the pain is worst and build the full documentation stack for that one part of your system. Written so a reviewer can load context in minutes, and so a Claude agent working in that area has what it needs without reading everything. This is the proof of concept.

Weeks 5–6

Handoff & Framework

I document the method — not just the deliverable, but how to extend it across the rest of your system. You get a repeatable framework your team can use going forward. I come back in six months to check what's changed.
